# Product

## Register

brand

The primary surface is the **public communication site** (homepage, berita, agenda, pengumuman, galeri, kepengurusan, divisi, program kerja, aspirasi, kontak) — design IS the product here. The protected `/admin` surface is treated as **product** register on a per-task basis.

## Users

**Primary**: HMTE members and TRE / SV UGM students who need quick, current information — agenda, announcements, articles, open recruitment, event updates, and organization news. They arrive looking for "what's happening in HMTE right now," often on mobile, between classes.

**Secondary**: lecturers and department stakeholders who need to see that HMTE communicates responsibly; alumni, partners, sponsors, and prospective students assessing HMTE's activity and credibility.

The job to be done: understand HMTE's current activity and find a specific piece of information (an agenda item, an announcement, an article) fast.

## Product Purpose

An official-but-lively HMTE (Himpunan Mahasiswa Teknik Elektro, Sekolah Vokasi UGM) website that functions as a **modern communication hub**, not a static organizational profile. It presents agenda, announcements, and articles as the homepage priority so the site feels active, updated, and useful. Success looks like: a student finds the agenda/announcement they came for in seconds, and a lecturer or partner comes away trusting that HMTE communicates credibly.

First implementation phase uses structured sample data (no CMS required yet), modeled so it can later be replaced by real content or a backend.

## Brand Personality

**Resmi tapi hidup** — official but alive. Three words: **credible, warm, student-led.**

Voice and tone: clear, responsible, warm, modern, student-led. Not stiff, not too wild. Borrows the TRE prototype's abstract-modern engineering visual language (large confident typography, dark hero, warm off-white sections, technical linework, navy + gold accent used sparingly) but speaks as a student organization communicating activity — never as a formal academic department.

Emotional goals: confidence and credibility for stakeholders; usefulness and momentum for students.

## Anti-references

- **Formal study-program / prodi messaging** — curriculum, SKS, accreditation as the main homepage story. This is the TRE *prodi* site, which HMTE must not be confused with.
- Generic campus website templates and ordinary card-grid layouts.
- Startup-like hype language; overly expressive student-festival aesthetics.
- Loud, chaotic, rebellious, meme-like, or overly playful visuals — "advisor-safe" is a hard constraint; an over-expressive style risks rejection by lecturers.
- Static organizational-profile feel where the homepage reads as a brochure rather than a live feed.

## Design Principles

1. **Active, not archival.** The homepage is a live feed (agenda → pengumuman → berita), never a static profile. Recency and "what's happening now" lead.
2. **Resmi tapi hidup.** Credible enough for lecturers and partners, unmistakably student-led. Every choice is checked against both audiences.
3. **Advisor-safe by default.** When a move risks reading as loud, chaotic, or unserious, choose the calmer option. Energy comes from typography, rhythm, and freshness — not from noise.
4. **Borrow the language, not the subject.** Use the TRE prototype's abstract-modern engineering visuals for *feel*; the subject is always HMTE organizational activity.
5. **Restraint as signal.** Navy + gold accent and technical linework are used sparingly so they read as deliberate signals, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (watch the navy/gold pairings and muted slate text on warm off-white), large text ≥3:1, full keyboard navigation with visible focus states, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation. Audience spans students, lecturers, and alumni across a wide device and connection range, so mobile-first, fast-loading, and legible-at-zoom are baseline expectations, not enhancements.
